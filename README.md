# Chopda (चोपड़ा)

## Summary

Chopda is a private invoice tracker for a small team. You define your own
step-by-step flows, then move every invoice through those steps with its
documents attached. The flow is the product — not a generic invoice form.

## Problem

Invoice work often lives in spreadsheets and folders on individual laptops.
That works for a handful of invoices and breaks down as volume grows:

- There is no single place to see where an invoice stands.
- Documents are scattered and hard to share.
- The process lives in people’s heads, and it differs by invoice type.
- It is hard to see who did what and when.

Most tools focus on creating and sending invoices. They do not help track a
custom process around paperwork you already have.

## Solution

Chopda lets the team write the checklist once as a reusable flow, then run
every invoice through it.

Each invoice shows its current step, who owns it, and what documents belong
to that step. History stays visible. Access is limited to signed-in users with
clear roles, so outsiders cannot browse the workspace.

## Features

- **Custom flows** — Build and publish step-by-step flows (for example Standard
  vs Courier). Running invoices keep the version they started on.
- **Invoice tracking** — Lists and a detail view answer “where is this
  invoice?” with progress, owner, and current step.
- **Documents on steps** — Files attach to the step that needed them and open
  only after a permission check.
- **History** — Each completed step records who finished it and when.
- **Roles** — Admin, Builder, and Viewer shape what people can see and change.
- **Dashboard** — Open, waiting, overdue, and completed counts, plus items that
  need attention.
- **Reminders** — In-app alerts when a step waits too long or payment is due.
- **Team and company** — Admin manages users; everyone has a profile; the team
  shares one company profile.
- **Safe deletes** — Soft delete with an undo window before permanent removal.
